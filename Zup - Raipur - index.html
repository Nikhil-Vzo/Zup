How it works

Book in 3 steps. Done by dinner.

No hunting for numbers, no repeated bargaining, no “bhaiya kab aaoge?” uncertainty.

1

Tell Zup the work

Pick a service, describe the job in one line, choose your Raipur area and time slot.

2

Get matched & see the price

3

Work done, then you pay

Questions, answered.

Is Zup live in Raipur yet?

Not yet — this is the upcoming showcase site. Join the waitlist above and you'll hear first when bookings open.

How do workers get paid?

Workers see their payout before accepting a job. Once you approve the finished work, payment is released to them — no chasing, no weeks of waiting.

Can I hire a worker for a full day or every week?

Yes. Book by the job, by the day, or on a regular schedule — for example a cook every weekday or a gardener every weekend.

Which areas of Raipur will you cover first?

Central Raipur first — Telibandha, Shankar Nagar, Pandri, Gudhiyari and nearby areas — then outward as verified workers join.

Is Zup like Zomato / Swiggy?

Same idea, different job: instead of ordering food, you order trusted help — a plumber, electrician, cook, driver or gardener — with clear pricing and ratings.
